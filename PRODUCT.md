# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Portfolio visitors and technical reviewers evaluating the homelab, with the owner as a secondary operational user.

## Product Purpose

Show that the applications and Kubernetes platform are healthy in real time, while retaining enough detail to investigate reliability problems.

## Brand Personality

Calm, technical, credible.

## Anti-references

Overcrowded stock Grafana dashboards, decorative NOC displays, and health states communicated by color alone.

## Design Principles

- Make overall health understandable in seconds.
- Show real operational evidence instead of decorative metrics.
- Reveal detail progressively rather than filling the first viewport.
- Keep status language and thresholds consistent across dashboards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Pair semantic colors with explicit text labels and preserve clear focus and reading order.
